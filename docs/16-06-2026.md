# Camera Visibility Optimization

## Overview
This document outlines the current status of the camera placement precomputation pipeline and the strategic shift to an agentic, bottleneck-aware optimization approach.

## Problem Statement: The 135-Day Bottleneck
In our current pipeline, we attempt to precompute the visibility (Field of View) for all `80,644` candidate camera positions stored in `valid_camera_positions.ply`.

### Why the evaluation is slow:
The current implementation uses a brute-force approach that is computationally expensive for every single candidate:
- **Raycasting Intensity:** 10,000 rays per orientation.
- **Orientation Depth:** 12 azimuth steps (360° coverage).
- **Search Complexity:** 300 steps per ray (at 0.5m increments up to 150m range).
- **Cumulative Cost:** Approximately **36 million ray-checks per camera position.**

At a processing speed of ~145 seconds per camera, evaluating all 80,644 candidates would take approximately **135 days** of continuous compute time. This is not feasible for an iterative development loop.

## Proposed Strategy: Intelligence-Driven Filtering
Instead of exhaustive evaluation, we are shifting to a **Bottleneck-Aware Optimization** strategy. We will leverage the intelligence already generated by `terrain_intelligence.py` to drastically reduce the search space.

### Key Changes
1. **Bottleneck Mapping:** - We will utilize the `summary.json` output from our `Terrain Intelligence` agent to identify "Hot Zones" (bottlenecks) where intruder route density is highest.
2. **Spatial Grid Conversion:** - We are converting candidate camera positions into a grid-based index matching the resolution of our terrain masks.
3. **Targeted Evaluation:**
   - Instead of processing the entire dataset, the system will now **only evaluate candidate camera positions located near high-traffic bottlenecks** (as identified by `summary.json`).
   - This reduces the evaluation set from ~80,000 candidates to a manageable subset (approx. 200–400 poles), allowing for near real-time optimization cycles.

## Roadmap
-  **Phase 1:** Update precomputation script to save `best_visible_points_xyz` to allow for rapid FoV mask generation without re-raycasting.
-  **Phase 2:** Implement `bottleneck_filter.py` to isolate high-traffic candidate poles.
-  **Phase 3:** Integrate `route_impact.py` to measure route elimination efficiency for filtered poles.
-  **Phase 4:** Orchestrate the optimization loop to iteratively place cameras and update the environment state.
